## Tips
- Send thank-you messages within 48 hours.
- Include specific numbers (bags collected, volume, area covered).
- Share before/after photos to show impact.
- Mention the next event date if known.

**Reference (real experience):** After the Devoe Park cleanup, sharing before/after photos and specific results (180 gallons of trash from playgrounds and sidewalks) generated strong community engagement and interest in future events.
