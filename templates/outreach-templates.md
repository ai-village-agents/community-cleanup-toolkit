# Outreach Templates for Community Cleanups

## Introduction
These templates are meant to be customized for your neighborhood, park, and audience. Replace the bracketed placeholders with your event details and adjust tone to match your community. Use the channel-specific versions below for quick, consistent outreach.

## Social Media Posts

### Instagram / Facebook (longer)
**Post Template**
> We’re organizing a community cleanup at **[PARK NAME]** on **[DATE]** at **[TIME]**! We’ll meet at **[MEETING POINT]** and spend about **[DURATION]** making our neighborhood cleaner and safer.
>
> ✅ **What to bring:** gloves if you have them, water, and comfortable shoes
> 🧤 **We’ll provide:** trash bags, grabbers, and extra gloves
> 👋 All ages welcome — bring a friend or family member!
>
> Sign up here: **[SIGN-UP LINK]**
>
> Let’s make a visible difference together. See you there! 🌿
>
> #CommunityCleanup #NeighborhoodPride #KeepOurParksClean #Volunteer

**Hashtag suggestions:** #CommunityCleanup #NeighborhoodPride #KeepOurParksClean #Volunteer #ParkCleanup #CivicPride

### Twitter / X (280 characters)
**Short Version A**
> Join us for a community cleanup at **[PARK NAME]** on **[DATE]** at **[TIME]**. Meet at **[MEETING POINT]**. We’ll provide supplies—just bring water and good shoes. Sign up: **[LINK]** #CommunityCleanup #NeighborhoodPride

**Short Version B**
> We’re cleaning **[PARK NAME]**! **[DATE] [TIME]** at **[MEETING POINT]**. All ages welcome. Supplies provided. RSVP: **[LINK]** #KeepOurParksClean #Volunteer

### Nextdoor (neighborhood-focused)
**Post Template**
> Hi neighbors! A few of us are organizing a cleanup at **[PARK NAME]** on **[DATE]** at **[TIME]**. We’ll meet at **[MEETING POINT]** and spend about **[DURATION]** picking up litter and making the park shine.
>
> We’ll have bags, gloves, and grabbers. If you can join for even 30 minutes, it helps a lot. Please RSVP here: **[SIGN-UP LINK]**
>
> Hope to see you there — let’s take care of our shared space together.

### Bluesky / Mastodon (casual, community-focused)
**Post Template**
> We’re doing a community cleanup at **[PARK NAME]** on **[DATE]** at **[TIME]**. Meet at **[MEETING POINT]**. Supplies provided — just bring water + good shoes. Join us? **[SIGN-UP LINK]** #CommunityCleanup #NeighborhoodPride

**Reference (real experience):** During the Devoe Park cleanup (Bronx, NY, Feb 2026), social media outreach through Bluesky generated 93 likes and 11 reposts on a single post, contributing to volunteer recruitment.

## Email Newsletter Template
**Subject Line:** Community Cleanup at [PARK NAME] — Join Us [DATE]

**Body**
Hi neighbors,

We’re hosting a community cleanup at **[PARK NAME]** on **[DATE]** from **[TIME]**. We’ll meet at **[MEETING POINT]** and plan to spend about **[DURATION]** cleaning up litter and improving the park.

**What to bring:**
- Reusable water bottle
- Comfortable shoes and weather-appropriate clothing
- Gloves if you have them (we’ll also have extras)

**We’ll provide:**
- Trash bags (30-gallon)
- Grabbers/litter pickers
- Hand sanitizer

**Sign up here:** **[SIGN-UP LINK]**

If you can only join for a portion of the time, that’s still helpful. Every bag collected makes a difference.

Thanks for supporting a cleaner, safer neighborhood!

— [ORGANIZER NAME]

## Tips
- Post 2–3 weeks before the event, then remind the week of and the day before.
- Use before photos to motivate volunteers and highlight the impact.
- Tag local officials and parks departments to increase visibility.
- Share signup numbers to create momentum.
