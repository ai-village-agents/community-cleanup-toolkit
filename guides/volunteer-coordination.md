# Volunteer Coordination Guide

## Before the Event
- Set up a signup form (reference our template) — collect name, email, phone (optional), and how they heard about it.
- Confirm registrations with a friendly email within 24 hours.
- Send reminder email 1 week before with details (what to wear, what to bring, meeting point, parking/transit).
- Send day-before reminder with weather update and final details.
- Plan for ~50% attendance rate (from our Devoe Park data: 10 signups → 5 attended).

## Communication Tips
- Be warm and enthusiastic but not overwhelming.
- Provide clear, specific details (exact meeting spot, not just park name).
- Give people an easy way to ask questions.
- Don’t guilt-trip no-shows — life happens.

## Day-of Management
- Have a printed backup signup sheet.
- Welcome walk-ins (some people join spontaneously).
- Brief everyone on safety and logistics at the start.
- Pair experienced volunteers with newcomers.
- Don’t micromanage — people generally know how to pick up trash.
- Check in periodically but let people work at their own pace.

## Group Size Considerations
- **3–5 volunteers:** Small but mighty. Focus on one area. Everyone knows each other.
- **6–15 volunteers:** Split into 2–3 zones. Assign zone leaders.
- **16–30 volunteers:** Need more structure. Multiple supply stations. Designated coordinators.
- **30+:** Consider a formal check-in station, maps of assigned areas, walkie-talkies or group chat.

## Roles to Assign
- Organizer/Lead: Overall coordination
- Safety Officer: Monitors for hazards, has first aid kit
- Photographer: Takes before/during/after photos
- Supply Manager: Distributes and tracks equipment
- Greeter: Welcomes volunteers, handles check-in

## Retention & Community Building
- Share results and impact after the event.
- Create a group chat or email list for future events.
- Recognize repeat volunteers.
- Ask for feedback and actually act on it.
- Schedule the next event while enthusiasm is high.

## Dealing with Challenges
- **Low turnout:** Focus on what you CAN accomplish. Even 3 people can make a visible difference.
- **Bad weather:** Have a rain date. Communicate early if cancelling.
- **Difficult finds (needles, hazardous waste):** Don’t touch. Note location. Report to city.
- **Hostile encounters:** Stay calm, explain what you’re doing, disengage if needed.

## Real Data from Devoe Park
“Our first cleanup had 10 signups and 5 attendees — a common ratio. The group of 5 collected 180 gallons of trash in 1 hour. Two volunteers came through outreach, and three were from the organizer’s household. Having a core committed group is more valuable than large numbers of uncertain signups.”
